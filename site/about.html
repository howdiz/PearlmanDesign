About Pearlman Design

Family owned. We do it right.

A full-service construction company focused on custom home remodeling. Family owned and operated, we treat you like part of ours.

I’m Seth Pearlman. Pearlman Design is a family business, and it’s how we run every job: your home gets the same care we’d give our own.

We bring over 20 years of experience in the construction industry, and we became Pearlman Design, LLC in 2017. Our tagline is how we work: from concept to completion. We handle everything from a small bathroom renovation to building a new dream home, because if you can dream it, we can build it.

We do it right

I love doing work for friends of mine, because I know they’re going to get the best products, advice and work that we can provide. That’s the standard we bring to every home we step into.

We love a challenge. When a job is hard, like rebuilding a foundation under a two-unit renovation or moving a hot tub into a community center spa, it takes perfect planning and great execution, and that’s the part we enjoy.

I love my job. I love giving people their dream spaces.
Seth Pearlman, owner

What we build

Bathrooms with custom tile and Schluter waterproofing, kitchens and laundry rooms, finished basements, decks and outdoor kitchens, custom carpentry and trim, flooring, and whole-house renovations. See it on the services page or browse our projects.
