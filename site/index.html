What we do

Full-service remodeling, start to finish

From a single bathroom to a whole-house renovation, one family-owned crew sees it through.

  • Bathrooms

    Master baths, steam showers, heated floors and custom tile, built on Schluter Systems waterproofing.

  • Kitchens & laundry

    Kitchen remodels and laundry rooms with custom cabinetry and finishes.

  • Basements

    Finished basements, including basement bathrooms and underpinning.

  • Whole-house & additions

    Full-home renovations and additions, including second-story work.

  • Decks & outdoor

    PVC and composite decks, cable rail, outdoor kitchens and hot tubs.

  • Custom carpentry

    Built-ins, mudrooms, bench seats, offices, wine walls and more.

  • Flooring & trim

    Hardwood, luxury vinyl plank and tile floors, plus board and batten, wainscoting and curved trim.

  • Light commercial

    Careful work for local businesses, from an audiology office to a community center spa.
